\begin{frame}{Introduction}
    Biodiversity loss is emerging as a material risk factor for financial markets, alongside the more familiar climate-change channel.

    The paper by Guidolin and Pedio (Review of Finance, 2062) asks a sharp question:

    \question{Do commodity markets price biodiversity-related transition risk? Do commodities with higher biodiversity exposure command a risk premium, and do they react differently to binding policy news?}

    The mechanism operates through two channels.
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Normal times}: higher biodiversity intensity: greater regulatory and reputational exposure: higher required excess return (risk premium).
    \item \textbf{Around the Kunming Declaration (Oct 2021)}: the event raises the salience of biodiversity transition risk, triggering a negative price revision for high-intensity commodities — to allow for larger future returns..
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
